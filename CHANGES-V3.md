# Portfolio Prototype V3 — Changes

## What's New in V3

### 1. New Tagline (Modern, Differentiated)
**Old:** "I turn ambiguous product problems into validated, shippable products."
**New:** "I build working products while others are still debating requirements."

**Why:** More confident, differentiated, positions you as a builder vs. slide-deck PM.

### 2. Completely Redesigned "How I Work" Section

#### New 4 Principles Grid:

**01 — Build to learn, don't spec to assume**
- Emphasizes AI-assisted prototyping (v0, Cursor, Claude)
- "2-hour coded prototype > 20-page PRD"

**02 — Discovery is continuous, not a phase**
- Weekly customer touchpoints, not one research sprint
- Assumption mapping + risk-first testing

**03 — AI accelerates everything** ⭐ NEW
- Shows you USE AI to build faster, not just build AI products
- Positions you as 2026 PM, not 2022 PM

**04 — Ship the smallest thing that teaches**
- MVP = hypothesis validation, not feature list
- Anti-scope-creep positioning

#### Updated Process Diagram:
- Added **"Prototype"** as Step 03 (between Discovery and Evidence)
- Shows you build to learn, not spec to assume
