How I work

I reduce risk through rapid validation, not documentation theater.

01

Build to learn, don't spec to assume

I prototype working demos—AI-generated UIs, clickable flows, functional MVPs—before writing requirements. A 2-hour coded prototype reveals more than a 20-page PRD.

02

Discovery is continuous, not a phase

User interviews, competitive teardowns, and technical feasibility checks happen weekly, not once. I map assumptions explicitly and test the riskiest ones first.

03

AI accelerates everything

I use AI to prototype faster (v0, Cursor, Claude), synthesize research, and generate test environments. AI isn't just a product domain—it's part of the workflow.

04

Ship the smallest thing that teaches

MVP means validating the core hypothesis, not shipping a feature list. If it doesn't reduce uncertainty, it's not an MVP—it's scope creep.
